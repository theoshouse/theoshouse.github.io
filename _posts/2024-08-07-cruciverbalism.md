I’m bad at doing crosswords. I can do the NYT’s Mondays and Tuesday, though Wednesday gets hard. Sometimes I’ll do the whole Sunday puzzle, but it’s no harder than a Wednesday puzzle. I’m not patient enough to practise daily or slog my way through slow puzzles. And I’m not smart or stubborn enough to solve cryptic puzzles, like the ones *Harper’s* publishes. But solving a really difficult clue or figuring out a clever theme is super gratifying, and writing your own crosswords is even more gratifying—and fun!

I’ve been doing this for a while, but my puzzles have a very small audience. I spend hours creating them, and only a couple people will ever solve them. So now I’m putting them on the internet, where they’ll live forever, probably in obscurity.

Here’s one I made a few years ago which you can [play online at this link](https://crosshare.org/crosswords/CPvC7cVr7INhW7uIAM11/very-old-crossword) or [print](https://crosshare.org/api/pdf/CPvC7cVr7INhW7uIAM11). I think it’s about as hard as a Wednesday NYT puzzle.
