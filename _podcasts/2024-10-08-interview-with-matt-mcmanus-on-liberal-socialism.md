I chat with [Matt McManus](https://lsa.umich.edu/polisci/people/lecturers/matt-mcmanus.html), an author and political science lecturer at the University of Michigan, to talk about his [book on liberal socialism](https://www.amazon.com/Political-Theory-Liberal-Socialism/dp/103264723X), the history of conservatism in the United States, and egalitarianism. This is an episode of MIR Meets from the [McGill International Review Podcast](https://www.mironline.ca/mir-meets-matt-mcmanus/).
