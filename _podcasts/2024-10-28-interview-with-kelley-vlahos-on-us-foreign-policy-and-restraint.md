I interviewed Kelley Beaucar Vlahos, the editorial director of *[Responsible Statecraft](https://responsiblestatecraft.org/)* and former executive editor of *The American Conservative*. We discussed the US foreign policy establishment and the emerging restraint movement, which seeks to reduce US military commitments around the world and orient foreign policy more around diplomacy than war.
